Game Developer & Unity XR Expert

Dharmik Gohil is a Unity game developer based in Surat, Gujarat, India — available for hire for game development, multiplayer systems, and AR/VR/XR projects. Currently a final-year B.Tech IT student at CHARUSAT University (DEPSTAR College), he combines academic rigour with hands-on production experience.

Dharmik currently works as a Jr. Game Developer at Qevaron Technology (Vadodara). He specializes in Unity 3D/2D game development, real-time multiplayer networking with Photon PUN, and immersive AR/VR/XR experiences using AR Foundation and XR Toolkit. He also builds full-stack web applications with React, Node.js, and MongoDB (MERN stack). His work spans mobile games, international client projects like Gain Live (Bangladesh Client Project), competitive multiplayer titles like Mavericks Battlegrounds (showcased at CHARUSAT Expo 3.0 to 5,000+ visitors), and XR training simulations.

Notable achievements include winning at IIT Bombay Techfest 2025, participating in Global Game Jam 2026, winning Hack-A-Vation and Hackron 2025 (DY Patil University). Dharmik co-founded the Indian Developer Community (IDC) with 150+ members. He holds certifications from NPTEL (DSA with Java), HackerRank, and has completed Web 3.0 and blockchain development internships.

Available for contracts, game developer internships, and remote projects. Free consultation — reach out via the contact page or email directly.
